Reflection

I believe Cyber Operations would be a good fit for me because I have always enjoyed solving technical problems, learning how computers and networks work, and figuring out how to protect systems from threats. Through my experiences with CyberPatriot, SkillsUSA, FBLA, and FIRST Robotics, I have developed both technical and problem-solving skills that connect directly to the field of cybersecurity.

One part of Cyber Operations that interests me is the combination of defense and hands-on technical work. I enjoy working through problems where I have to investigate what is happening, find weaknesses, and figure out how to fix them. My experience with CyberPatriot has especially helped me become more comfortable working with Windows and Linux systems and thinking about cybersecurity in high-pressure situations. I have also competed in cybersecurity-related events, which has shown me that I genuinely enjoy this type of work rather than just being interested in it as a career.

Cyber Operations also fits my long-term goals. I am interested in pursuing a career in penetration-testing or cyber defense, where I could use technical skills to identify vulnerabilities, protect systems, and respond to security threats. I like that the field is constantly changing because it means there will always be something new to learn. I do not want a career where I am doing the exact same thing every day, and Cyber Operations would give me opportunities to continue developing my skills.

Overall, I think Cyber Operations would be a strong fit for me because it combines my interests in technology, cybersecurity, problem-solving, and continuous learning. My experiences so far have already given me a foundation in these areas, and I would like to continue building on that foundation through further education and hands-on experience.

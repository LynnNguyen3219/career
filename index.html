Cyber Operations focuses on protecting computer networks, systems, and information from cyber threats. Cyber operations professionals monitor networks for suspicious activity, investigate security incidents, identify vulnerabilities, and implement defensive measures to prevent attacks. They may also conduct threat analysis, digital forensics, security assessments, and incident response. The field requires strong problem-solving, technical, and analytical skills, along with knowledge of networking, operating systems, cybersecurity tools, and emerging threats. Cyber operations careers can be found across government, military, technology, finance, healthcare, and other industries where protecting digital infrastructure and sensitive information is essential.
